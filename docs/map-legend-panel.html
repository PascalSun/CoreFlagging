Commodity colours and symbols

A solid rim is the hole's own record: WA from the DMIRS HyLogger drillhole package (DASC), South Australia from SARIG, Victoria from the Earth Resources bores database. A dashed rim (≈ in the list) is an inference, because the survey records no target on the hole: Queensland and the NT take the commodities of the exploration report or permit the hole belongs to; NSW and Tasmania take the nearest known mineral deposit within 5 km (Geoscience Australia), the project's namesake first. The colours are CoreTrace's own, with the chemical symbol (Au, Cu, Ni…) on each mark so the colour need not be remembered.

Design notes

This page is a mock-up of the map's key. The marks are drawn by the same canvas code as the app (shapes.tsx), and the commodity colours and symbols come from TARGET_COLOUR unchanged. The holes, core types and years are made up: the holes are placed round the Eastern Goldfields, and their mix is invented so that every class has holes to show.

Layout

  1. The key is a single column pinned to the left edge. It runs from under the top bar to the bottom of the map, scrolls inside, and folds down to its title (⤡).
  2. The zoom buttons move to the bottom right, stacked over the basemap switch. In the app they keep the basemap's offset, panelW + chatW.
  3. The scale bar stays as Mapbox's bottom-left control, pushed right with a margin of the panel's width.
  4. Sections from top to bottom: Core type · Target commodity · Where the target comes from · Model reading · Year drilled · Context layers.

  • The grid replaces "Holes that crowd". Grouping, with its donut clusters, goes, and so does the switch in the key.
  • The holes are binned into square cells of a fixed size on the ground: about 40 km at the switch, doubling with each step out. A cell takes the colour of the commodity most of its holes were drilled for, a named commodity ahead of Other and Unknown. Its opacity is that commodity's share of the cell, so a mixed cell is paler. Hovering over a cell lists its top four commodities.
  • The filters apply: "Show only this" on Gold leaves only the cells with gold holes.
  • A key for the grid appears over the scale bar only while the map is in that view.
  • In the app the cells are computed in the browser from the hole list, which is already loaded, and drawn as a GeoJSON fill layer with maxzoom at the switch, recomputed when the filters change. An alternative is H3 hexagons, if square cells look too much like a raster.
  • This shows what was drilled for, not prospectivity. A true prospectivity map needs a score per cell (from a model, or from the platform's prospectivity scoring), which can later fill the same cells.

Rows

  • Each row shows a mark, a name and a count of real holes, whatever is in view. The counts are faceted: a section counts the holes that pass the other sections' filters. Its own filter is left out, so a hidden row still says how many holes it would bring back. "Show only this" on Plagioclase only, for example, turns every other section into a breakdown of those holes. The year histogram follows the same rule.
  • Raw-scan holes have no core type. They show while every core type is on, and drop out as soon as any core type is hidden. Showing only Plagioclase only gives exactly its 39 holes, and "Raw scan only" falls to 0.
  • A row whose count falls to 0 under the other filters is greyed. Rows keep the order of the State's totals, so they do not jump as the counts change.
  • On hover, two actions float over the right end of the row: Show only this and Hide (or Show once the row is hidden). They cover the name and the count rather than pushing them aside. "Show only this" a second time brings every row back.
  • The filters combine across sections.

What changes

  • new An inferred target is a hatched fill instead of a dashed rim. It shows on the dot as well as on the badge.
  • new "Where the target comes from" is keyed with a plain white disc and a hatched white disc. "Model reading" is keyed with the two badge shapes in neutral grey. Neither carries a symbol, so no commodity is implied.
  • new Other commodities and Target unknown explain themselves through an ⓘ next to the name.
  • new A raw-scan badge is a rounded square, so the shape survives zooming in.
  • new The "Colour by" picker goes. The colour is always the commodity, and core type becomes a filter section in the style of a bar list.
  • new Year drilled is a histogram with a two-handled range.
  • new Zoomed out to a 200 km scale, the holes become a commodity grid (see above).
  • new The marks in the key are drawn flat, with no shadow, as the fill marks are. On the map they keep the shadow that lifts them off the imagery.
  • kept Every commodity colour and symbol, and How the colours are chosen.

Needed from the data

  • api Year drilled. drill_date is in the hole's meta (app/pipeline/preprocess.py), but HoleRow does not carry it. The hole list needs a drilled year, or null.
  • api Core type is worked out from h.strip, as mainType does now. No new field is needed.
